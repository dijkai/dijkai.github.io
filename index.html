// 01 — Philosophy

A dijk is a Dutch dike. We build defenses together.

Five principles, borrowed from how the Dutch live with water.

/01

Build before the flood

Study AI risks before a disaster forces the lesson.

/02

Defense in depth

Layered safeguards across design, security, evaluation, deployment and law.

/03

Shared stewardship

Safety is kept by many people, not owned by one lab or government.

/04

Engineering meets governance

Technical research connected to law and policy, including the EU AI Act.

/05

Consensus through dialogue

We welcome disagreement and work toward common ground.
